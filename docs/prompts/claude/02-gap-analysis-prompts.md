# Claude Gap Analysis Prompts

Purpose:
- Identify coverage gaps, technical debt, and missing capabilities in the AI-enabled Open Banking QE platform.

When to use:
- Reviewing test coverage and compliance gaps
- Assessing missing AI or MCP capabilities
- Prioritizing improvements in security, performance, or accessibility

Expected output:
- A concise list of coverage or architecture gaps
- Priority-ranked recommendations
- Specific areas for remediation and follow-up

Prompt examples:
- "Analyze the current QE platform and identify gaps in CDR contract validation and accessibility testing."
- "List missing capabilities in the AI scenario generation pipeline for Open Banking QA."
- "Recommend highest-priority improvements for performance, security, and reporting coverage."
